About DataBridge

Why we built DataBridge

Independent data collection is often the biggest bottleneck in student research. Designing instruments, traveling to field sites, and enumerating can consume months a degree timeline doesn't have, and small self-collected samples limit how far a thesis's analysis can go.

DataBridge takes a different approach: instead of a single team curating datasets, students share the data they've already collected with each other. Upload your dataset once, and it's discoverable and downloadable by other student researchers working on related topics, and vice versa.

How It Works

  1. Create a free account.
  2. Upload your dataset with a title, category, description, region, and year.
  3. Your dataset is immediately searchable and downloadable by other logged-in students.
  4. Search the catalog to find datasets other students have shared.

Your Responsibility as an Uploader

When you upload a dataset, you're confirming you have the right to share it and that it doesn't contain information that could identify individuals without their consent. See Guidelines & FAQ for the full data-use terms.

Early stage: DataBridge is a new platform. Expect the catalog to grow as more students join and share their data.
