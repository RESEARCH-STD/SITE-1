About SST

Who We Are

Sustainable Solutions Tanzania (SST) is a research, technology, and innovation company committed to creating practical solutions for people, businesses, institutions, and the environment. We work with the public and private sectors, academia, development partners, and communities to turn knowledge and ideas into sustainable impact.

TakwimuBridge: our student data-sharing tool

One way we put this into practice is TakwimuBridge, a platform where students create accounts, upload the research data they've already collected, and search & download data shared by other students, so less time goes into repeating fieldwork and more into analysis. See our Products page for details, or go straight to the catalog.

Your Responsibility as a TakwimuBridge Uploader

When you upload a dataset, you're confirming you have the right to share it and that it doesn't contain information that could identify individuals without their consent. See Guidelines & FAQ for the full data-use terms.
