Guidelines & FAQ

Data-Use Guidelines & FAQ

Data-Use Terms

  1. Academic, non-commercial use only. Datasets on this portal are for coursework, theses, dissertations, and non-commercial research.
  2. Only upload data you have the right to share. By uploading, you confirm the dataset is your own work or you have permission to share it.
  3. No personally identifying information. Do not upload data that could identify individual survey respondents without their informed consent.
  4. Attribution. When you use a dataset from the catalog, credit the uploading student and note it was obtained via TakwimuBridge.
  5. No redistribution. Don't re-publish raw files you've downloaded outside your own research team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to browse the catalog?

No, anyone can search and view dataset details. You need a free account to upload a dataset or download one.

Is my upload reviewed before it appears in the catalog?

No, uploads are published immediately so the catalog reflects what students are actively sharing.

Can I edit or remove something I uploaded?

Yes, go to "My Uploads" from your account menu to edit the details or delete a dataset you no longer want shared.

What file types can I upload?

CSV, Excel (.xlsx/.xls), JSON, Stata (.dta), SPSS (.sav), ZIP, or PDF (for documentation), up to 20MB per file.

I found a dataset that looks wrong or inappropriate. What do I do?

Let us know via the Contact page so we can review it.

Is there a cost to use TakwimuBridge?

No, the catalog is free to use. See Our Services for paid advisory/analysis support offerings.
