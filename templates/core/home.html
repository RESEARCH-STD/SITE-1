About SST

Who We Are

Sustainable Solutions Tanzania (SST) is a research, technology, and innovation company committed to creating practical solutions for people, businesses, institutions, and the environment. We work with the public and private sectors, academia, development partners, and communities to turn knowledge and ideas into sustainable impact.

Our Services

What We Do

We provide integrated services to help organizations achieve their development goals.

Data Collection Advisory Services

Guidance on designing survey instruments, sampling strategy, enumerator training, and field logistics for your own primary data collection.

Data Analysis Support Services

Help with cleaning, structuring, and analyzing your dataset: statistical methods, software support, and interpreting results for your research questions.
