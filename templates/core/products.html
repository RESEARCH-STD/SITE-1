TakwimuBridge

Student Data-Sharing Tool

A platform where students create accounts, upload the research data they've collected, and search & download datasets shared by other students, so less time goes into repeating fieldwork and more into analysis.

  • Search and filter a growing catalog of shared datasets
  • Upload your own data with a title, category, and description
  • Download datasets shared by others once logged in
  • Manage everything you've uploaded from one place
