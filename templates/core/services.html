Our Services

Beyond the catalog

In addition to the student data-sharing catalog, our team offers the following support services for research projects.

Data Collection Advisory Services

Guidance on designing survey instruments, sampling strategy, enumerator training, and field logistics for your own primary data collection.

Data Analysis Support Services

Help with cleaning, structuring, and analyzing your dataset: statistical methods, software support, and interpreting results for your research questions.

Disclaimer: These are advisory and support services only. Any academic write-up (thesis chapters, dissertations, reports, or other submissions) is handed over to the client in full, and remains the client's own work and sole responsibility. We do not write or submit academic work on a client's behalf.
